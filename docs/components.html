Mobbin Design System.

An engineered reference library of real product interfaces. Strictly monochrome near-black ink (#141414) on pure white canvas (#ffffff), structured by a ladder of neutral tints, stadium pills (rounded.full), and variable weights.

1. Colors & Neutral Tint Ladder.

Shadow-free elevation carried by fill differences and 1px hairlines. Electric Blue is reserved exclusively for commercial decisions.

Ink Black
--colors-ink: #141414

Primary CTA pills, display headings, polarity-inverted footer.

Electric Blue
--colors-accent: #0066ff

The ONLY chromatic accent. Reserved for "Popular" badges & savings.

Canvas
--colors-canvas: #ffffff

Default page background, standard cards, and active toggle pills.

Soft Canvas (6%)
--colors-canvas-soft: #f3f3f3

Workhorse tint: floating nav-pill, featured cards, FAQ rows.

Field (8%)
--colors-field: #f0f0f0

Fill for form inputs, borderless at rest with 2px ink focus ring.

Hairline (16%)
--colors-hairline: #e0e0e0

1px control border for outlined buttons and resting cards.

2. Typography & Variable Weights.

Set in Inter / Saans with signature variable weights: 650 for chunky headings with 1.0–1.13 line-height, 450 for running text, and airy 300 for lead subtitles. Zero letter-spacing everywhere.

6. Design System Principles (Do's & Don'ts).

Core design governance rules directly from the Mobbin design lead specification.

✓ DO

  • Gallery-white canvas: Keep #ffffff canvas and let imported content supply all saturation.
  • Stadium pills: Use rounded.full for every button, nav pill, badge, and toggle.
  • Variable weights: Set 650 for chunky headings with 1.0–1.13 leading and terminal periods.
  • Scarcity of blue: Reserve #0066ff strictly for commercial decisions (Popular badge & savings).
  • iOS Squircles: Render icons with 30% corner radius.
  • Polarity Inversion: Close pages with the near-black #141414 footer rounded at 24px top.

✕ DON'T

  • No drop shadows: Elevation is built solely by tint fills and 1px hairlines.
  • No blue CTAs: Primary actions are always ink black #141414 pills, never electric blue.
  • No rectangular buttons: Square-cornered buttons do not exist in Mobbin.
  • No borders on resting fields: Form inputs are #f0f0f0 fills; 2px ink ring only on focus.
  • No letter-spacing: Let typography run naturally at 0 letter-spacing.
  • No extra colors: Do not introduce random green, purple, or decorative gradients.
